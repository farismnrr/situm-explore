- Native/mobile remains out of scope.
- User explicitly wants low complexity and no premature architecture.
- Existing PostgreSQL must be explored by Codex before migrations; unrelated schemas/tables must not be modified.
- Situm credentials will be supplied locally through environment configuration and must not be committed.
