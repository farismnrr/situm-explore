Product

Everything important, without dashboard clutter.

The UI keeps the indoor map primary while the rest of Situm’s operational data stays easy to reach when you need it.

Map Viewer workspace

Switch buildings and floors, search POIs, pick locations, preview routes, favorites, realtime overlays and trajectories.

◇

Cartography explorer

Browse buildings, floors, POIs, categories, geofences and path networks from one consistent information model.

Realtime operations

See current people and device locations, online state, building context and live movement with clean operational status.

↗

Directions & navigation

Build routes between indoor points, compare accessible routing options and surface step-by-step directions.

▥

Analytics & reports

Explore visitors, heatmaps, geofence stay time, positioning time, user positions and map viewer usage.

⚙

Organization context

Keep user, group, alarm, viewer configuration and organization metadata visible without turning the POC into an admin suite.

Operations

Map-first by design.

The map carries the spatial task. Everything else supports it: POI discovery, route planning, realtime position overlays, geofence context and trajectory playback.

Analytics

Operational answers, not vanity charts.

Reports are grouped around concrete questions: who visited, where people stayed, how long positioning was active, where activity concentrates, and how the map viewer is used.
